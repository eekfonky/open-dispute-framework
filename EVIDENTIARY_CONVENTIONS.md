# Evidentiary Standards & Case Conventions

This document establishes the strict evidentiary rules, citation anchors, and data quarantine standards governing all operations within the Open Dispute Framework.

---

## 1. The Evidentiary Tiering System
Every fact entered into the dispute record must be tagged with exactly one evidentiary tier:

* **Tier 1 (T1) — Contemporaneous Record:** Created at or near the time of the event (e.g. emails sent that day, diary notes made immediately after an incident, instant messages, official minutes).
* **Tier 2 (T2) — Recollection:** Witness or claimant statements prepared after the event from memory.
* **Tier 3 (T3) — Employer Assertion:** Uncorroborated employer or HR claims.
* **Tier A (A) — Legal Analysis & Inference:** Interpretation or breach arguments. **Never in raw facts**; lives only in briefing/analysis documents and must cite facts by `[EV-XXX]` ID.

---

## 2. Citation Anchors & Stable Identifiers
* **Documents:** `[DOC-XX]` — sequential at ingestion (e.g. `[DOC-01, p. 3]`).
* **Events:** `[EV-XXX]` — sequential chronological events in `case/log.md` or chronology tables.
* **Without-Prejudice Materials:** `[WP-XX]` — strictly quarantined in `without_prejudice/`.

---

## 3. Without-Prejudice (WP) Quarantine
Settlement offers, protected severance discussions (e.g. ERA 1996 s.111A), and *without prejudice* correspondence must be strictly quarantined in `without_prejudice/`.
* **Zero Contamination:** Never cite `[WP-XX]` content in open grievance letters or tribunal claim drafts.
* Segregate first, resolve legal admissibility later with a qualified solicitor.

---

## 4. Evidence Protection & Counter-Charge Prevention
* **Lawful Possession:** Only use materials the worker receives or holds in the ordinary course of their work.
* **No Bulk Forwarding / Data Breaches:** Never recommend bulk-forwarding confidential employer files to personal inboxes (this gives the employer grounds for gross misconduct counter-charges).
* **The Clean Route (DSAR):** Use a formal Subject Access Request (under UK GDPR / regional privacy law) to lawfully compel the employer to provide the worker's emails, notes, and records.
