# Scottish Employment Law: Core Statutory Framework

## Primary Legislation & Principles
* **Employment Rights Act 1996 (ERA 1996)**:
  * **s.47B**: Protected disclosures (Whistleblowing) detriment — no service requirement, uncapped compensation.
  * **s.103A**: Automatic unfair dismissal for making protected disclosures — no service requirement, uncapped compensation.
  * **s.128**: Interim relief fuse — application must be lodged within **7 calendar days** of dismissal.
  * **s.203**: Statutory restrictions on contracting out (Settlement Agreements require independent relevant legal adviser).
  * **s.43J**: Contractual terms (including settlement/confidentiality clauses) precluding protected disclosures are void.
* **Equality Act 2010**: Unlawful discrimination, harassment, victimisation, and failure to make reasonable adjustments.
* **Contract & Delict**:
  * Scots law of contract applies (distinction in unilateral promises and contract formation).
  * Workplace psychiatric injury / stress falls under the law of **delict** (*Prescription and Limitation (Scotland) Act 1973* — triennial 3-year limitation).

## Prescribed Persons (Whistleblowing)
* **Care & Residential Services**: Care Inspectorate (*Social Care and Social Work Improvement Scotland - SCSWIS*).
* **Social Services Workers**: Scottish Social Services Council (*SSSC*).
* **General Health & Safety**: Health and Safety Executive (*HSE*).
* **Data Protection & Privacy**: Information Commissioner's Office (*ICO*).

## Tribunal & Dispute Body
* **Tribunal**: Employment Tribunals (Scotland) — Edinburgh & Glasgow registry.
* **Pre-Claim Requirement**: ACAS Early Conciliation mandatory before presenting ET1.
* **Limitation**: 3 months less 1 day from the effective date of termination or last act of detriment.
