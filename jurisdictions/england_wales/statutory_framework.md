# England & Wales Employment Law: Core Statutory Framework

## Primary Legislation & Principles
* **Employment Rights Act 1996 (ERA 1996)**:
  * **s.47B**: Protected disclosures (Whistleblowing) detriment.
  * **s.103A**: Automatic unfair dismissal for whistleblowing.
  * **s.128**: Interim relief application fuse (7 calendar days).
  * **s.203**: Settlement agreements statutory requirements.
* **Equality Act 2010**: Protected characteristics and statutory obligations.
* **Common Law & Tort**:
  * English common law of contract.
  * Personal injury and workplace psychiatric injury governed by tort law (*Limitation Act 1980* — 3-year limitation for personal injury).

## Prescribed Persons (Whistleblowing)
* **Care & Health**: Care Quality Commission (*CQC*).
* **Social Work**: Social Work England.
* **Education**: Office for Standards in Education, Children's Services and Skills (*Ofsted*).
* **Financial Services**: Financial Conduct Authority (*FCA*) & Prudential Regulation Authority (*PRA*).

## Tribunal & Dispute Body
* **Tribunal**: Employment Tribunals (England & Wales).
* **Pre-Claim Requirement**: Mandatory ACAS Early Conciliation.
* **Limitation**: 3 months less 1 day from termination or last act of detriment.
