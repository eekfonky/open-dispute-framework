# Evidence & Documents Folder

Place your open case files here. The AI agent will read, cite, and cross-reference them using standard citation anchors (e.g. `[DOC-01, p. 2]`).

### What to gather and place here:
1. **Employment Contract:** (or Written Statement of Employment Particulars) — to verify notice period, job title, hours, and pay clauses.
2. **Staff Handbook / Workplace Policies:** Specifically the Grievance Policy, Disciplinary Procedure, and Sickness/Absence Policy.
3. **Recent Payslips:** (Last 3 months) — to verify hourly rate, contracted hours, and gross monthly pay.
4. **Contemporaneous Evidence (T1):**
   * Emails sent/received at the time of incidents.
   * WhatsApp / text message screenshots.
   * Personal diary entries or contemporaneous notes written on the day.
   * Medical / GP fit notes for work-related stress.

> ⚠️ **DO NOT PLACE PROTECTED SETTLEMENT OFFERS HERE:**
> Any letters or emails marked *"Without Prejudice"* or *"Protected Conversation under s.111A"* must be placed in the `without_prejudice/` folder instead to prevent evidentiary contamination.
